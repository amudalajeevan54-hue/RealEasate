Our Journey

The Jeevan Real Estate Brand

Founded on a vision to simplify luxury real-estate acquisitions, Jeevan Real Estate has grown to become India's premier boutique property brokerage. We bridge the gap between architectural masterpiece searchers and clean-titled property holders, ensuring transactions remain completely stress-free.

With corporate offices in Mumbai, Bangalore, and Goa, we service high-net-worth individuals, expats, and enterprises looking for high-quality residential, coastal, and commercial assets. Our customer-focused approach dictates that we represent only physically verified properties under transparent negotiations.
